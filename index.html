Course Goals

1. Consider technology’s role in acts of making

These projects showed me that code and structure directly shape meaning. The way pages are organized affects how users understand content, proving technology is not neutral but actively influences communication.

2. Embrace experimentation

I explored non-linear design and interaction instead of traditional formats, pushing myself to think creatively about how media can function.

3. Engage with others in a workshop environment

My original idea changed after realizing it lacked clarity, leading me to shift toward a stronger narrative approach.

4. Practice revision

I reworked layout and structure multiple times, improving clarity and cohesion across projects.

5. Presentation

Publishing online made me focus on usability and how others interact with my work.

Final Project

This film follows a woman dealing with loss and isolation, told entirely through visuals. I used lighting, editing, and pacing instead of dialogue to communicate emotion. Shooting in a cemetery during day and night created contrast, while filming in 4K allowed me to control detail and color in editing. This project reflects my growth in visual storytelling and understanding how technical choices shape meaning.
