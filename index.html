Final Project

Short Film – Narrative Without Dialogue

This project is a short narrative film that follows a woman dealing with loss, financial pressure, and isolation. The story is told entirely without dialogue, relying on visuals, lighting, and editing to communicate emotion and progression. I focused on how scenes connect through transitions and pacing, using music and visual tone to guide the viewer. Shooting in locations like a cemetery during both day and night allowed me to create contrast and reinforce emotional shifts. I filmed this using a FujiFilm XT5 in 4K and adjusted color and lighting in editing to match the mood of each scene. This project pushed me to rely on visual storytelling instead of explanation.

Course Goals

Technology:
Technology shaped every part of these videos, from filming decisions to editing choices that affected pacing and meaning.

Experimentation:
Each project used a different format, allowing me to explore observation, memory, and open-ended questioning.

Workshop:
My work shows a shift toward clearer structure and stronger visual communication over time.

Revision:
I reworked clips, timing, and structure multiple times to improve flow and meaning.

Presentation:
Uploading to YouTube made me focus on clarity and how the audience experiences the work.

Reflection

This course changed how I approach creative work. I moved from focusing mainly on ideas to focusing on how those ideas are experienced visually and emotionally. My final film reflects that shift, where I used lighting, camera angles, and editing to tell a story without dialogue. I learned that small technical decisions can have a major impact on how a project feels and how it is understood.
