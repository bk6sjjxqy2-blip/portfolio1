Portfolio Statement

This portfolio brings together a series of digital media projects exploring code, sound, image, and interaction. Across these works, I focus on experimentation, remixing, and process-based creation, drawing on ideas from Derrida, Barthes, Benjamin, and Debord.

Course Goals

1. Consider technology’s role in acts of making
Code and web structures shape how meaning is created and experienced.

2. Embrace experimentation
These projects prioritize play, remixing, and non-linear design.

3. Engage with others in a workshop environment
Feedback influenced structure and interaction.

4. Practice revision within and across media forms
Projects evolved through continuous revision of code and design.

5. Learn to present your work
Work is presented through interactive web experiences.
