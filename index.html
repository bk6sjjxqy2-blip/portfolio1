Final Project

Video currently private:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_rfh4-6SVoA

This short film follows a woman dealing with loss, financial pressure, and isolation. The story is told entirely without dialogue, relying on visuals, lighting, and editing to communicate emotion and progression. I focused heavily on how scenes connect through transitions and pacing, using music and tone to guide the viewer. Shooting in locations like a cemetery during both day and night allowed me to create contrast and reinforce emotional shifts. Filming in 4K with my FujiFilm XT5 gave me control over detail and color, which I refined in editing to match each scene’s mood. This project represents my strongest work in the course because it shows my ability to combine technical skills with visual storytelling, using images instead of dialogue to communicate meaning.
